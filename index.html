About this map

This map shows the 3D positions of real galaxies from three public redshift surveys. Distances are worked out from each galaxy's redshift using the standard cosmological model (Planck 2018 parameters), as comoving distance in megaparsecs. 1 Mpc = 3.26 million light-years. The lookback time is how long the light took to reach us. The most distant galaxies here are seen as they were about 9.6 billion years ago.

Data sources (REAL data)

  • 2MASS Redshift Survey (2MRS): Huchra et al. 2012, ApJS 199, 26, via VizieR (CDS) J/ApJS/199/26. Covers the whole sky out to about 300 Mpc (43,105 galaxies, cz > 1000 km/s).
  • SDSS DR17 main galaxy sample: RA 130-235°, Dec 0-55°, 0.01<z<0.09 (183,012 galaxies), via SkyServer (skyserver.sdss.org/dr17).
  • DESI Data Release 1 large-scale-structure clustering catalogues v1.5, northern galactic cap only: BGS_BRIGHT-21.5 (z 0.1-0.4), LRG (z 0.4-1.1) and ELG_LOPnotqso (z 1.1-1.6), from data.desi.lbl.gov/public/dr1/. The map uses a subset: BGS 217,614, LRG 180,000, ELG 100,000.

By default the map shows 100,000 galaxies (for speed on phones). Tick "load all" for about 724,000.

Accuracy notes - please read

  • Redshift-space distortions: distances come from redshift, which includes each galaxy's own motion. Galaxy clusters therefore look stretched along our line of sight ("Fingers of God"), and walls look slightly squashed.
  • Survey footprints: the surveys cover only parts of the sky. The empty regions are areas that weren't observed, not empty space. The band of sky behind our own Milky Way is also hidden by dust (the "Zone of Avoidance").
  • Selection: each survey picks particular galaxy types and brightness limits. Distant galaxies appear sparser because only the brightest are seen, so dot density does not equal true density.
  • Downsampling: only a random subset of each survey is drawn. Counts are listed above.
  • Named structures: cluster centres come from published positions (NED and literature). The "Great Wall" markers show only rough centres of very large, extended structures. We checked that every marker sits on a real overdensity of galaxies in this data.
  • Extra layers: the filament web is computed from real SDSS data with a tidal-field (Hessian) method. The PREDICTED layer is a statistical model (a fractal mock) and does not show real galaxies. The CONCEPTUAL lattice is art, not science.
  • The scale bar is correct at the centre of the view; perspective changes it nearer or further away.
